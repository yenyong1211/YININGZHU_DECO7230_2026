# Chinese VR Prototype Test Results

## 1. Participants and Testing Context

Five participants tested the prototype on 21 and 23 October 2026. They had different levels of Chinese and VR experience.

| Participant | Date | Chinese Experience | VR Experience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | 21 October | None | None |
| P2 | 21 October | None | A little |
| P3 | 23 October | A little | Frequent use |
| P4 | 23 October | A few previously learned words | A little |
| P5 | 23 October | Some experience; level unspecified | Some experience; frequency unspecified |

The records state that controller instructions were added for the 23 October sessions. The sessions therefore cover two stages of development rather than identical testing conditions. Differences in performance cannot be attributed solely to the instructions. Complete session durations were not recorded.

## 5. Facilitator Assistance

Assistance is distinguished from errors and game-generated feedback. An incorrect action does not itself establish that a facilitator prompt occurred.

| Level | Definition | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H0 | No facilitator assistance | The participant acts without a prompt or demonstration. |
| H1 | Neutral prompt or facilitator-provided instruction replay | “Try again.” |
| H2 | Operational guidance | Explaining the grab button or reminding the participant to release the book. |
| H3 | Direct answer or demonstration | Identifying the correct target or demonstrating the solution. |

| Participant | Task 1 Assistance | Task 2 Assistance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | One H1 prompt explicitly recorded. | Grab and release guidance, consistent with H2. Prompt count not recorded. |
| P2 | H0: no assistance recorded. | Explanation of the grab control, consistent with H2. Prompt count not recorded. |
| P3 | H0 during the timed task. A separate prompt helped locate Start Task beforehand. | Three prompts recorded; levels cannot be determined because prompt content was unspecified. |
| P4 | H0: no assistance recorded. | Assistance not clearly recorded. The held-book wait does not establish whether a prompt was given. |
| P5 | H0: no assistance recorded. | A retry prompt, consistent with H1. The exact prompt count was not explicitly stated. |

Except where a level was explicitly entered, classifications above were assigned retrospectively from the recorded prompt content. Unspecified prompts remain unclassified. “No assistance recorded” describes the source entry, rather than independently verifying the absence of help.

Independent completion requires completion without facilitator assistance. Assisted completion requires both assistance and confirmed completion. Receiving help alone is not sufficient to classify an attempt as assisted completion. Ambiguous outcomes prevent a reliable overall independent-completion rate.

## 6. Oral Vocabulary Checks

Before the VR experience, the facilitator pronounced the six Chinese words and checked whether participants understood their meanings. The check was repeated after the experience. A correct meaning received one point, with a maximum score of six.

These were live oral checks outside the VR tasks. They assessed immediate understanding of spoken word meanings, not written-character recognition or comprehension directly demonstrated during gameplay. Repeating a pronunciation alone would not demonstrate understanding of its meaning. The original sheet's audio-only description should not be interpreted as evidence that standardised recordings were used for these checks.

### Participant Scores

| Participant | Before / 6 | After / 6 | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| P1 | 0 | 4 | +4 |
| P2 | 0 | 5 | +5 |
| P3 | 2 | 5 | +3 |
| P4 | 2 | 6 | +4 |
| P5 | 3 | 6 | +3 |
| Mean | 1.4 | 5.2 | +3.8 |

All five participants scored higher after the experience. P4 and P5 answered all six meanings correctly in the post-check. P1 and P2 began with no correct answers and subsequently answered four and five correctly.

### Results by Word

| Word | Meaning | Correct Before | Correct After |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 书 | Book | 3/5 | 4/5 |
| 电脑 | Computer | 1/5 | 5/5 |
| 书包 | School bag | 3/5 | 5/5 |
| 找到 | Find | 0/5 | 5/5 |
| 拿起 | Pick up | 0/5 | 3/5 |
| 放进 | Put into | 0/5 | 4/5 |

电脑, 书包 and 找到 were understood by all five participants in the post-check. 拿起 had the lowest post-check accuracy; P1 and P3 did not identify its meaning correctly.

These changes describe immediate performance after the experience. Without a control condition or delayed test, they do not establish that VR or grabbing caused learning, or that the words were retained over time.

Grab-and-place received the lowest mean rating. Feedback received the highest rating, although observations documented uncertainty about release. The final item measures perceived learning support rather than objective learning effectiveness. These individual items are not a validated composite scale.

## 9. Recording Limitations

- Several outcomes describe incorrect actions without clearly recording the final task result.
- P1's Task 2 numeric error count differs from the events described.
- Assistance levels and prompt counts were not consistently recorded; retrospective classification is limited to clear descriptions.
- P4's Task 1 error count is blank and has not been treated as zero.
- Full session duration, audio replays, card revisits and language-support use were not consistently recorded.
- Controller instructions changed between the two session dates, and participants differed in prior experience.
- Live pronunciation may have varied; standardised delivery, repeat counts and timing of oral checks were not established by the records.
- Vocabulary was not directly assessed during the VR tasks, and no delayed retention test or control condition was included.

Missing values have not been replaced with assumed values, and uncertain outcomes have not been converted into successful completions.
