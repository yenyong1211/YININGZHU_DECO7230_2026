# Duolingo XR — Testing Results

## Interactive Prototype 1

## 1. Testing Summary

The usability testing was conducted with **4 participants**.

The original target was approximately 5 participants, but only 4 participants were tested within the available testing time.

The testing focused on:

- discovering how to interact with the Study Room;
- using WASD and mouse controls;
- clicking 3D learning objects;
- viewing Chinese, pinyin, and English vocabulary;
- using pronunciation audio;
- remembering short-term vocabulary-object associations.

## 4. Key Observations

Across the four participants:

- All participants required at least one hint during the interaction.
- Three out of four participants successfully opened all five learning objects.
- Participant 2 opened 4 out of 5 objects.
- Participants generally rated the interaction, vocabulary UI, and pronunciation audio positively.
- Audio received the highest rating, with all four participants rating it 5/5.
- Three participants directly mentioned that the interaction needed clearer instructions or guidance.
- Camera control was confusing for at least one participant, particularly the need to hold the right mouse button to look around.
- One participant commented that the vocabulary card colour blended too much with the Study Room environment.
- Participants generally felt that connecting vocabulary with 3D objects helped them remember the words.
- Short-term vocabulary recognition varied between participants.

---

## 5. Main Usability Issues Identified

### Lack of Initial Instructions

Several participants were unsure what to do when first entering the Study Room.

Possible improvement:

- add a short onboarding guide;
- display WASD movement instructions;
- show mouse-look instructions;
- explain that objects can be clicked.

### Camera Controls

The right-mouse camera control was not immediately obvious.

Possible improvement:

> `Hold Right Mouse Button + Move Mouse = Look Around`

This instruction could remain visible during the first stage of the experience.

### Vocabulary Card Visibility

One participant reported that the vocabulary card was too similar in colour to the environment.

Possible improvement:

- increase contrast;
- use a clearer background colour;
- make Chinese text larger;
- visually separate Chinese, pinyin, and English.

### Interaction Guidance

Participants suggested adding more prompts and interactive feedback.

Possible future improvements include:

- floor-based movement instructions;
- wall instructions;
- object highlights;
- visual prompts;
- sound feedback.

---

## 6. Testing Limitation

Only **4 participants** were tested during this session.

The original target was approximately 5 participants, but there was not enough time to complete a fifth test.

In the next testing session, I will improve the testing process and time management so that more participants can be tested within the available session.
